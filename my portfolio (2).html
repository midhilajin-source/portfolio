Key Insights

What the figures above show, read directly from the reports.

Traffic and search visibility
  • Direct was the largest channel with 4,023 sessions (79.21%). Organic Search was second with 893 sessions (17.58%).
  • Engagement is the clearest area to improve: a 14.2% engagement rate and 7s average engagement time per session.
  • Search Console recorded 458K impressions but 1.49K clicks (0.3% CTR) at an average position of 29.4, so the largest gain is moving pages onto page one.
Rankings and on-page
  • SEMrush visibility is 8.21% (+0.99%), with 5 keywords in the top 3 and 12 in the top 10.
  • 44 keywords rank in the top 100. Most of them are not yet in the top 10, which is where the next ranking gains sit.
  • The On Page SEO Checker identified 107 ideas across 16 pages. Content (63) and Semantic (14) ideas make up most of them.

Campaign Insights

Observations taken or calculated directly from the reporting-period data above.

Reporting period summary

  • Generated 912 leads during the reporting period.
  • Managed ₹75,237.49 in advertising spend.
  • Delivered 745,127 impressions.
  • Reached 196,449 users/accounts.
  • Overall average cost per lead was ₹82.50.
  • Campaign-level cost per lead ranged from ₹58.62 to ₹128.62.

Campaign-level observations

  • Used Car Loan Leads — Campaign 1 generated the most leads: 353 (38.7% of all leads) at ₹69.08 per lead.
  • Unsecured Business Loan — Campaign 1 had the lowest cost per lead at ₹58.62, generating 201 leads from ₹11,782.83 of spend.
  • Three of six campaigns delivered a cost per lead below the ₹82.50 average: Used Car Loan Campaign 1 (₹69.08), Unsecured Business Loan Campaign 1 (₹58.62) and Unsecured Business Loan Campaign 2 (₹70.28).
  • Top Up Car Loan Leads campaigns recorded the highest cost per lead: ₹128.62 (Campaign 1) and ₹102.86 (Campaign 2).
  • The two largest campaigns by spend accounted for 63.9% of total spend (₹48,049.78) and 58.9% of leads (537 of 912).
